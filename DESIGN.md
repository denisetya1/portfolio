# Deni Setyawan Portfolio Direction

Reading: an interactive portfolio for recruiters, founders, and engineering collaborators, expressed through a bold technical-editorial visual language.

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

## Identity

- The system-map motif connects Deni's roles, projects, and career milestones because his work centers on architecture and systems that coordinate many parts.
- Near-black and warm paper create an engineering-document contrast; acid green marks decisions and active states, while signal orange appears only in high-value highlights.
- Avenir Next supports clear, human reading. Arial Black gives the hero a decisive silhouette without using decorative monospace.
- The asymmetrical layout reflects a career that spans leadership, product engineering, platforms, mobile, games, and AI.
- Motion reveals relationships as visitors scroll and gives immediate feedback to filters and toggles. It never loops or competes with the content.
- Cards appear only for distinct projects. Their varied sizing reflects project scope instead of forcing every item into the same visual weight.
- Spacing alternates between dense evidence and open transitions so each section has a distinct rhythm.
- The site uses text initials rather than inventing a logo or portrait.
